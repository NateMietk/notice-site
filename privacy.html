Notice

Privacy policy · effective October 18, 2026

Notice is a journal for your evenings, nights and mornings. This page says plainly what the app does with your information. The short version: it stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

What you type or tap into the app: your nightly entries, weekly reviews, experiments, practices, thought records, and the settings you choose. If you connect Apple Health, the app also stores the values it read for each night (sleep times, wakings, naps, steps, workouts, time in daylight, heart rate and resting heart rate) so it can show them beside your entries.

Where it is kept

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Notice has no account system and no server. It does not send your entries, your Health data, or anything else to us or to anyone. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party software that could collect data.

Apple Health

Notice asks for read-only access to Apple Health, and only for the types it uses. It never writes to Health. What it reads is used only to fill in your own log and to show you patterns, on the device. Health data is never uploaded, and never stored in iCloud by the app. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to send. The app can export your log as a CSV file and can generate a PDF brief for a therapist or prescriber. Both go through the iPhone's share sheet to the destination you pick (Files, Mail, AirDrop, print). Nothing is sent automatically.

The "If things feel urgent" page contains links to crisis services (such as 988 and findahelpline.com). Tapping one opens the Phone, Messages or Safari app; those services have their own policies.

Optional lock

You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open the app. Authentication is handled by iOS; the app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Deleting your data

Delete any night from within the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores. Any CSV or PDF you exported lives wherever you saved it.

Children

Notice is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes how the app handles your information (for example, an optional iCloud sync of your own entries between your devices), this page will be updated before that version is released, with the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: noticeapp.support@gmail.com.

Notice is a record, not medical advice, and not a crisis service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
